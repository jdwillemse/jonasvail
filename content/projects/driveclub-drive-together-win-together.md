#DRIVECLUB was not your typical racing game. It wasn’t just about cars or famous tracks, or even just about winning races. It was about driving in a club made up of your friends, and winning _together_. The strategy was to highlight this unique aspect of the game by showing a group of friends pledging allegiance to their driving club.
