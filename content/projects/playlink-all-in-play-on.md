PlayLink was a new series of PlayStation games launched in 2017, that people could play on their PS4 using their smartphone or tablet as a controller. The strategy was to show a new, much broader audience that just because they don’t consider themselves “gamers”, they too can have fun and _PLAY_ on PS4 with PlayLink.
