We worked with global pharmaceutical company, Lilly, to refine and simplify their brand and product value proposition for their diabetes portfolio in the UK. We developed a brand positioning that was centered around simplifying the process for both health care providers and patients, and partnering with patients at every step of the disease. We created a product portfolio campaign off the back of it that spoke to patients in an easy to understand, and very human way.
