To help fight tooth and nail against new, mega-competitors PlayStation and Xbox, we developed a campaign to try to re‐establish Sega within the video gaming category and launch their newest (and last) major hardware platform, the Sega Dreamcast, in the US on 9-9-99\. The strategy was to position Dreamcast as being so advanced that its AI could outsmart you – making it the ultimate gaming competitor.
